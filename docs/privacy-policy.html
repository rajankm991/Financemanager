Summary

Finance Ledger keeps everything you enter on your own phone. We do not run a server, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect, upload, sell or share your data. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.

Who we are

Finance Ledger is published by Rajan Mishra. Contact: rajan.km991@gmail.com.

What the app stores

The transactions, cashflow entries, salary details, budgets, categories and notes you type into the app, and the payment alerts you approve. This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We never receive it.

Notification access (optional)

If you choose to turn on notification access, the app reads the text of notifications posted by your payment, banking and messaging apps (currently Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED, ICICI iMobile, YONO SBI, BHIM SBI Pay, slice, and your SMS app). The app keeps a notification only if it contains an amount in rupees and a payment word such as debited, credited, paid or received. All other notifications are ignored immediately and are not saved.

Kept alerts are turned into a pending item (date, amount, merchant and the original message text) that appears in the app's Inbox. Nothing is added to your records until you approve it. This processing happens entirely on your device. The information is not uploaded, shared, sold or used for advertising. You can switch notification access off at any time in Android Settings under Notification access, and you can delete any pending item by rejecting it.

Exports and backups

If you use Export to Excel or Save to Google Drive, the app creates a file and hands it to Android's share sheet or file picker. You decide where it goes. We do not receive the file.

Fonts

The app loads its text fonts from Google Fonts when you are online. Google may receive your IP address and device information as part of that request, as described in Google's privacy policy. The app works without it, using fallback fonts.

Permissions

Notification access (only if you enable it) to read payment alerts as described above. Internet access, used only to load fonts and for sharing features you start yourself.

Your choices and deleting your data

All data is on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings deletes it. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it for you, so use Export to Excel to keep a backup.

Children

Finance Ledger is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
