Finance Ledger - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Summary

Finance Ledger keeps what you enter on your own phone or in your own browser. There is no developer server and no user account, and the developer never receives your data. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. If you choose to turn on Google Drive sync, a copy of your ledger is kept in your own Google Drive (see below). Without sync, nothing leaves your device.

Who we are

Finance Ledger is published by Rajan Mishra. Contact: rajankm991ai@gmail.com.

What the app stores

The transactions, cashflow entries, salary details, budgets, categories and notes you type into the app, the payment alerts waiting in your Inbox, and the alerts you approve. By default this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device (or in your browser for the web version). We never receive it.

Notification access (Android, optional)

If you choose to turn on notification access, the app reads the text of notifications posted by your payment, banking and messaging apps (currently Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED, BHIM, ICICI iMobile, HDFC Bank, Axis Mobile, Kotak, YONO SBI, BHIM SBI Pay, slice, and your SMS app). The app keeps a notification only if it contains an amount in rupees and a payment word such as debited, credited, paid or received. All other notifications are ignored immediately and are not saved. One-time-password messages are discarded.

Kept alerts are turned into a pending item (date, amount, merchant and the original message text) that appears in the app's Inbox. Nothing is added to your records until you approve it. This processing happens on your device. The information is not shared, sold or used for advertising. It is not uploaded anywhere by the app, except as part of your own ledger file if you turn on Google Drive sync, in which case your Inbox, including the original message text, is saved in your Google Drive with the rest of your ledger. You can switch notification access off at any time in Android Settings under Notification access, and you can delete any pending item by rejecting it.

Google sign-in and Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app asks for one permission, drive.file, which lets the app create and open only the files it makes itself. The app uses it to keep one file, Finance_Ledger_Sync.json, in your own Google Drive so the same data appears on the web app and the Android app, and for Excel exports you ask it to save there. The app cannot see your other Drive files, emails, contacts or any other Google data.

Your data goes directly between your device and your own Google Drive. It is not sent to the developer or to any other server, and it is not shared, sold or used for advertising or analytics. Google handles the file under Google's own privacy policy.

Exports

If you use Export to Excel, the app creates a file and hands it to Android's share sheet or file picker. You decide where it goes. We do not receive the file.

Fonts

The app loads its text fonts from Google Fonts when you are online. Google may receive your IP address and device information as part of that request, as described in Google's privacy policy. The app works without it, using fallback fonts.

Permissions

Notification access (only if you enable it) to read payment alerts as described above. Internet access, used to load fonts, for Google sign-in and Drive sync if you turn them on, and for sharing features you start yourself.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn off sync at any time in Settings, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete Finance_Ledger_Sync.json from your Google Drive to remove the synced copy.
  • Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage in Android Settings, or using "Clear everything" in the app's Settings deletes the data on the device.
  • We hold no copy, so we cannot recover your data for you. Use Export to Excel to keep a backup.

Children

Finance Ledger is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
