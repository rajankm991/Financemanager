**Full description** (max 4000):

```
Finance Ledger is a private money tracker that lives entirely on your phone.

Record income, expenses and investments, see where your money goes, and keep budgets, salary details and money lent or borrowed in one place. There is no sign-up, no ads, and nothing is uploaded: your data stays on your device.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Dashboard: income, expenses, investments, balance and savings rate for any month or year, with charts and yearly comparison.
- Transactions: add, edit, search and filter by month, type or category, with payment mode and notes.
- Cashflow: keep track of money you gave to or received from friends and family, with a running balance per person.
- Salary: month-by-month pay structure with automatic carry-forward.
- Budgets and categories: set monthly budgets and see how you are doing.
- Excel backup: export everything to an Excel file and import it back whenever you like.

INBOX FOR PAYMENT ALERTS (OPTIONAL)
Turn on notification access and Finance Ledger reads payment alerts from apps such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED, ICICI iMobile, YONO SBI, BHIM SBI Pay, slice and your SMS app. It picks out the date, amount and merchant and puts each one in your Inbox with a notes box. Nothing is added to your records until you approve it, and you can reject anything. Only alerts that contain an amount and a payment word are kept; every other notification is ignored and never saved. All of this happens on your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No account, no server, no ads, no tracking.
- Your data is stored only on your device.
- Notification access is optional and can be switched off at any time in Android settings.

Note: Finance Ledger is a tracking tool. It does not move money, make payments or give financial advice.
```
