Finance Ledger - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Finance Ledger is a personal finance app that runs in your web browser and as an Android app. This page explains what happens to your data.

What the app stores

Your transactions, cashflow entries, salary records, categories and settings. By default this data stays only on your own device (in the browser or app storage).

Google sign-in and Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app asks for one permission:

  • drive.file - lets the app create and open only the files it makes itself. The app uses it to keep one file, Finance_Ledger_Sync.json, in your own Google Drive, so the same data appears on the web app and the Android app (and for Excel exports you ask for).

The app cannot see your other Drive files, emails, contacts or any other Google data.

Who can see your data

Only you. Your data goes directly between your device and your own Google Drive. It is not sent to the developer or to any other server, and it is not shared, sold or used for advertising or analytics.

Notification capture (Android only, optional)

If you allow notification access in the Android app, payment alerts are read on the phone to suggest entries for your Inbox. They are processed on the device and are not uploaded anywhere by the app, except as part of your own ledger file if you turn on Drive sync.

Your control

  • Turn off sync any time in Settings, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete Finance_Ledger_Sync.json from your Drive, or use "Clear everything" in the app's Settings to remove local data.
